    Questions, answered.

    What ShotFix checks, how it scores, and what you get back.

    01What is ShotFix?

    ShotFix is an AI video quality check, or video QC, that you run before you publish. You upload a video with its brief, and ShotFix watches and listens to the whole cut. It returns a score out of 100, a publish verdict, six category scores and a list of findings, each pinned to a time range in the video with the frame that shows it.

    02How does ShotFix score a video?

    It scores six categories: consistency, visual quality, emotion and engagement, script and content, viral potential, and compliance. Each category has a weight and a confidence, and the weighted scores make the overall score. Under 50 is major rework, 50 to 69 needs work, 70 to 84 minor fixes, and 85 and up is ready. An open critical finding holds the verdict at needs work until it is fixed or dismissed.

    03What problems can it find in a video?

    Continuity breaks, where a face, an outfit, a product's colour or a voice changes between shots. AI artefacts such as garbled lettering, warping and morphing. Unsupported claims and industry rules for regulated categories. Platform fit: shape, resolution and length for each place you post. Measured signals: black frames, freezes and loudness. And the hook, the timing of the call to action, and pacing.

    04Does ShotFix work on AI-generated video?

    Yes. It checks for the problems generated footage tends to have: a character or product that drifts between shots, text that renders as gibberish, and shapes that warp or morph. Your reference notes count as ground truth, so if the brief says the can is coral, every shot is checked for coral.

    05What is the difference between Fast and Detailed analysis?

    Fast is a quick review: about three AI checks run in parallel and return scores and findings, for 1 credit per started video minute. Detailed runs the full pipeline and adds fixes and suggestions to review, for 3 credits per started video minute. You pick the mode for each analysis.

    06Which platforms does it check a video for?

    The platforms you list in the brief. For each one, ShotFix checks the shape, resolution and length of the video, for example whether TikTok and Instagram Reels get a 9:16 cut and YouTube gets 16:9.

    07Can I share a report with a client?

    Yes. A report can be shared as a read-only link that expires after 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days. Clients see the score, findings and suggestions without an account, and never the video file. Reports also export as HTML or JSON.

    08What happens after I fix the video?

    Run the analysis again. Earlier runs stay in History, so you can compare them. In the report you can confirm, dismiss or reopen findings and add notes, and approve, reject or mark suggestions as applied.

    09Who is ShotFix for?

    AI video creators deciding whether a video is ready to post on YouTube, TikTok or Reels. Agencies and studios reviewing client and ad work and sharing reports with clients. Brand and compliance reviewers checking claims, disclaimers and platform rules before a campaign goes live.

    10How do I get access to ShotFix?

    ShotFix is open to a small group of teams while it is tested. Request access with your work email and tell us what you make. Analyses are paid in credits per started video minute.
